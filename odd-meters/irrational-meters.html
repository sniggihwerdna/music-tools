Irrational Meters v7

Bars in ordinary meters (/4, /8) chained with bars counted in triplet units (/6 triplet quarters, /12 triplet eighths, /3 triplet halves) or quintuplet units (/5, /10). The quarter note stays the same speed throughout. A soft quarter-note click plays underneath and restarts at every bar line, so it never drifts across an odd bar; the unusual groupings are the challenge.

    How to read the units: a whole note splits into 4 quarters, 8 eighths, 16 sixteenths, or in triplets 3 thirds, 6 sixths (triplet quarters) or 12 twelfths (triplet eighths), or in quintuplets 5 fifths or 10 tenths. So a triplet quarter is ⅔ of a beat, and 5/6 lasts 3⅓ beats; 7/12 lasts 2⅓ beats. Groupings like 3+2/8 set the accents; plain numbers get a sensible default.
