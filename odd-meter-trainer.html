Odd Meter Trainer v12

Short bars of 3, 4 and 5 combined into a cycle like 3+5+4, or a single odd meter. A count-in, then it loops until you skip or stop. A steady pulse ticks every eighth; pitched notes mark the downbeat (highest), each group start (middle) and the other eighths (low).

Tempo

One beat = one eighth note (each tick). Start plays at the starting BPM; Skip to next picks a random tempo in the range. Set both the same to keep one tempo.

Sound

No sound on an iPhone? Turn the volume up and tap Start again. The page tries to play through the silent switch, but older iOS versions may still need the switch off.
