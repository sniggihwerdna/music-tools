Music Tools

Odd meters

Odd Meter TrainerShort bars of 3, 4 and 5 combined into cycles like 3+5+4, looped. Highest note on the downbeat, middle on each group start, low on the rest. Irrational MetersBars like 5/6, 7/12 and 2/3 (triplet units) chained with ordinary 4/4 and 3/8, over a steady quarter-note guide. Presets, your own sequence, or random. 5/4 GrooveA looping 5/4 drum groove: 3+2, 2+3 or 2+2+1, jazz or rock feel, with optional silent bars to test your time. Odd GroovesLight electronic grooves in 9/8, 5/4, 11/8, 13/8 and 15/8: pads, plucks, bass and shaker, with a choice of groupings. Over the Bar LineA steady 4/4 click with a grouping pattern (notes + space, or two of them) sliding across the bar lines until they line up again.

Time trainers

Rhythm TrainerA random rhythm over a metronome, then rest bars to play it back. Loops the same rhythm. Advanced Tempo Trainer (Higgins model)A new random rhythm at a new random tempo every round, with rest bars to play it back. Tempo DriftA metronome that speeds up and slows down by 0.1–3% per beat over a set cycle, with a randomizer and a tempo graph. Off-Grid Rhythm TrainerA downbeat plus 2–5 ticks placed between the grid, never on a 16th or a triplet. Metronome or musical mode, random tempos, 1- or 2-bar loops.

Vocal exercises

Quick MirrorTap to start singing, tap Done. It plays your take back, then records again, over and over. Audio MirrorSing and hear your take straight back, over and over. Quick mode needs nothing; loop mode sings along with a phrase from a file or the mic. Vocal Exercise PatternsA random string of notes (steps up to a major 7th, within an octave) moved up through all 12 keys and back down. Steady tone for singing along. Wide Interval TrainerRandom strings of wide leaps (4th and up by default) over two octaves, moved through the keys at a slower tempo. Microtonal Ear TrainerEndless random pitches in 12, 24, 36 or 72 notes per octave over a low drone, for unusual intervals. Sine or piano.

Sound organization

Retrograde TrainerA random phrase, 4 clicks, then the same phrase backwards. Test mode gives you a turn to reverse it first. Inversions too. Inversion TrainerA random phrase, 4 clicks, then the same phrase upside down. Choose the mirror note; test mode; 12-tone row with its inversion. Chord PlayerRandom 7th chords on organ or piano, one per bar over a click. Loop a set, or go endless for sing-along pitch finding. Call & Response ImprovA swung phrase of random, never-repeating notes, then the rest of the bar and one more to answer. Slide notes and rhythm from in (scale, swung 8ths) to out (chromatic, off-grid).
