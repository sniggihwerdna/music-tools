Music Tools

Quick MirrorTap to start singing, tap Done. It plays your take back, then records again, over and over. Audio MirrorSing and hear your take straight back, over and over. Quick mode needs nothing; loop mode sings along with a phrase from a file or the mic. Chord PlayerRandom 7th chords on organ or piano, one per bar over a click. Loop a set, or go endless for sing-along pitch finding. Microtonal Ear TrainerEndless random pitches in 12, 24, 36 or 72 notes per octave over a low drone, for unusual intervals. Sine or piano. 5/4 GrooveA looping 5/4 drum groove: 3+2, 2+3 or 2+2+1, jazz or rock feel, with optional silent bars to test your time. Vocal Exercise PatternsA random string of notes (steps up to a major 7th, within an octave) moved up through all 12 keys and back down. Steady tone for singing along. Wide Interval TrainerRandom strings of wide leaps (4th and up by default) over two octaves, moved through the keys at a slower tempo. Rhythm TrainerA random rhythm over a metronome, then rest bars to play it back. Loops the same rhythm. Advanced Tempo Trainer (Higgins model)A new random rhythm at a new random tempo every round, with rest bars to play it back. Odd Meter TrainerShort bars of 3, 4 and 5 combined into cycles like 3+5+4, looped. Highest note on the downbeat, middle on each group start, low on the rest. Retrograde TrainerA random phrase, 4 clicks, then the same phrase backwards. Test mode gives you a turn to reverse it first. Inversions too. Inversion TrainerA random phrase, 4 clicks, then the same phrase upside down. Choose the mirror note; test mode; 12-tone row with its inversion.
