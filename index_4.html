Music Tools

Vocal Exercise PatternsA random string of notes moved up through all 12 keys and back down, then a new string. Steady reference tone for singing along. Rhythm TrainerA random rhythm over a metronome, then rest bars to play it back. Loops the same rhythm. Advanced Tempo Trainer (Higgins model)A new random rhythm at a new random tempo every round, with rest bars to play it back. Odd Meter TrainerShort bars of 3, 4 and 5 combined into cycles like 3+5+4, looped. Highest note on the downbeat, middle on each group start, low on the rest. Retrograde TrainerA random phrase, 4 clicks, then the same phrase backwards. Test mode gives you a turn to reverse it first. Inversions too.
