Tempo Drift v5

A metronome whose tempo creeps up and down by a small percentage every beat, so you learn to follow a moving pulse. Three modes: a smooth drift up and back, random jumps within a range, or a drift that may reverse every 4 bars.

Each change lands somewhere in your range, at least 3 bpm away from the last tempo. With the accent set to divide evenly (for example accent every 4, change every 8), each change lands on an accented click. Changes to the range apply at the next jump.

Tempo changes by your rate every click. Every 4 bars (4 × the accent setting, or 16 clicks with no accent) a coin flip decides: keep going the same way, or reverse.

Tempo stays between 30 and 300 bpm; if a drift would go past either, it turns around. Changes apply from the next cycle, except volume.
